# Voice Note to Clean Transcript

Record a long voice note, get a clean transcript you can post from or turn into an article.

## The Unique Kernel
Long, conversational voice notes, like an interview, go in; a cleaned-up transcript that's ready to reuse comes out. Most transcription tools stop at a raw transcript. This one is built for the step after: getting the words ready to post and publish.

## Who It's For
The learner, first: someone who records long voice notes, such as interviews or talking through an idea, and needs usable text to post about or publish as an article. Today they search for apps, then clean the transcript by hand.

## The Core Loop
Record (or upload) a voice note, wait for the transcript, read the cleaned version, and copy it out. They come back each time they have a recording worth turning into text.

## Inspiration & Identity
Simple and quiet: one screen, one job. No features that aren't about getting clean text.

## Why This Matters to the Learner
They needed to transcribe a long interview voice note, clean it, post about it, and document it for an article. They had to search for tools to do it, and they want that workflow in one place.

## What "Working" Looks Like
Someone opens the app, records a voice note (a few minutes or longer), and sees the transcript appear with the filler and messy parts removed. The "that's cool" beat is watching a rambling recording come out as clean, readable text.

## The POC Boundary
- Record a voice note in the browser, or upload an audio file
- Transcribe it with an AI speech-to-text model
- Clean the transcript with an LLM: remove filler and messy parts, keep the meaning
- Show the cleaned transcript on screen, with a way to copy it

## Later
- Talk back with the model about the transcript
- Turn the transcript into a post or article draft
- Save and revisit past transcripts

## Explicitly Cut
- Accounts and sign-in: a single-user experiment doesn't need them
- Post or article generation: the app stops at a clean transcript for now
- Publishing or sharing integrations: copying text is enough for a proof of concept
